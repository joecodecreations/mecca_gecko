How to play

  1. Start a party (or join with a code / Find Friends).
  2. When you are a hider, paint your gecko to match a surface and freeze in place.
  3. When you are a seeker, hunt with a toy paint blaster. No radar — use your eyes.
  4. Rounds are short. Bots fill empty slots so a solo player or a two-person party still has a match.

FAQ

When can I download Mecca Gecko?

Mecca Gecko is preparing for its iPhone and iPad launch. Check the launch section for availability.

How do I invite friends?

Create a lobby and share the party code, or use Find Friends if you are already connected. Everyone needs the app installed.

What can I buy?

All launch maps are free. The shop offers individual characters and emotes, plus consumable diamond packs. Diamonds can also buy single-use powerups. Details: Shop.

I paid but my item did not arrive

For a character or emote bought through Apple, open Settings → General → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Diamond packs cannot be restored. If a payment is still missing, contact support with the item name and purchase date; never send passwords or full payment details.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Bots fill the room so you can practice painting, hiding, and seeking.

Microphone permission

Optional proximity / party voice. Decline the prompt if you do not want voice. The game still plays.

Report, mute, or block another player

During a party, open Settings → General → Player safety. Choose the player to mute voice, report a reason, or block. A guest who blocks leaves the party; a host removes the blocked player. Blocks and mutes are saved on your device. If reporting is unavailable or fails, email support with the party code, approximate time, and what happened. Do not include passwords or private installation credentials.

Replays and exported videos

In versions with Clip Studio, recording is optional and starts off. Enable Record future matches in Settings → Game → Replay recording to record future matches. A red REC · REPLAY indicator appears while recording. Turn the setting off to stop and discard the current recording. Replays contain the characters, names, paint, movement, and events your device saw; they do not contain microphone or voice-chat audio. Open Clip Studio to delete recordings or in-app exported videos. Deleting an original does not remove copies saved to Photos, Files, or shared elsewhere.

The game is huge / download failed

The world is full of props. Use Wi‑Fi, keep a few gigabytes free, and retry. If it keeps failing, tell us your device model.

Something crashed

Email joseph@codecreations.net with the time it happened. If you can, Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data, and attach the latest crash.

Delete my data / close an account

See Privacy. No separate Mecca Gecko account is needed. Email us to request access to or deletion of information we hold. Apple purchases stay on your Apple ID (that is Apple’s ledger, not ours).
