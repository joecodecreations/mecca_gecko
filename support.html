How to play

  1. Start a party (or join with a code / Find Friends).
  2. When you are a hider, paint your gecko to match a surface and freeze in place.
  3. When you are a seeker, hunt with a toy paint blaster. No radar — use your eyes.
  4. Rounds are short. Bots fill empty slots so a solo reviewer or a two-person party still has a match.

FAQ

The App Store listing / this site is the official support page

Yes. Apple asks for a working Support URL. This page is it. We read every email sent to joseph@codecreations.net.

How do I invite friends?

Create a lobby and share the party code, or use Find Friends if you are already connected. Everyone needs the app installed.

What does Upgrade ($5.99) unlock?

A one-time purchase. Extra maps and extra features as we ship them. It is not a subscription and it never makes you harder to spot or a stronger seeker. Details: Upgrade.

I paid but Upgrade did not unlock

On the device, open the shop / Upgrade screen and use Apple’s Restore Purchases. Still stuck? Email us from the Apple ID you used to buy.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Bots fill the room so you can practice painting, hiding, and seeking.

Microphone permission

Optional proximity / party voice. Decline the prompt if you do not want voice. The game still plays.

The game is huge / download failed

The world is full of props. Use Wi‑Fi, keep a few gigabytes free, and retry. If it keeps failing, tell us your device model.

Something crashed

Email joseph@codecreations.net with the time it happened. If you can, Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data, and attach the latest crash.

Delete my data / close an account

See Privacy. Email us and we will delete what we hold. Apple purchases stay on your Apple ID (that is Apple’s ledger, not ours).

App Review notes

Reviewers: you can start a match from the main menu → Play. Enable bots if you are the only player. No account is required for the core loop. Upgrade is optional and uses Apple’s sandbox IAP.
