Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy covers the Mecca Gecko iOS/iPadOS and Android apps and the website at meccagecko.com, provided by Valux Games LLC (“we”, “us”), 127 W Hargett St Ste 301 PMB 119, Raleigh, NC 27601, USA. Contact: support@valuxgames.com.

1. The short version

We built a party game, not an ad network. We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party ads. Payments go through Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android. Multiplayer uses a player host or, for protected internet matches, a dedicated game server, so people in your match can see what you chose to share in that match (display name, painted gecko, voice if you turn it on). Version 0.3.0 does not send product analytics. Starting with the next update, the apps send gameplay analytics to our analytics provider, GameAnalytics, so we can see how the game is played and fix problems.

2. Information we may have

  • What you type in the game — display name, party codes, optional feedback.
  • Gameplay in a match — paint, poses, positions. This is exchanged between player devices, passing through our relay for internet parties. We do not run a permanent world database of your hides.
  • Optional voice — if you allow the microphone, nearby / party voice is processed so others in the match can hear you. We do not record or store voice on our servers.
  • Purchases — Optional characters, emotes, and consumable diamond packs are billed by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. The store sends a receipt / entitlement so the app can unlock content. We never see your full card number. We do not receive your payment card number from Apple or Google. The item bought and its list price are also recorded in product analytics (below).
  • Online parties — Google Cloud Run relays gameplay and voice traffic, and Firestore holds short-lived lobby listings (party name, player names, counts, map, code, and host network address). Service infrastructure may retain IP addresses, request metadata, and diagnostic logs. Public listings are visible to other players.
  • Safety — a random installation credential authenticates your connection; its hash identifies your installation to the party and service. Saved blocks and voice mutes stay on your device. Player reports include installation identifiers, party code, a reason category, and timestamps; moderators can act on them. Reports do not record voice.
  • Optional local replays — in versions that offer replays, recording is off until you enable it. A replay saves the match as seen by your device, including player display names, character appearances, paint, positions, poses, and tag events. It does not record your screen, microphone, or voice chat. The app does not upload replay files to our servers. Your device backup settings and any destination you choose for export/share control copies outside the app. Other players may also save or share replays of a match you join.
  • Game Center — when you sign in, Apple processes your Game Center identity, eligible leaderboard scores, and achievement progress. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible according to your Apple privacy settings. The app stores progress and pending reports locally to retry interrupted submissions.
  • Support email — if you write us, we keep the thread long enough to help you.
  • Diagnostics — if you share analytics with Apple, we may receive crash logs and coarse device type so we can fix bugs. Google Play may collect its own install and crash diagnostics under Google’s terms. Starting with the update after version 0.3.0, the apps also report errors and crashes to GameAnalytics (below).
  • Product analytics (GameAnalytics) — starting with the update after version 0.3.0 (version 0.3.0 sends no analytics), the app shows a one-time notice before it sends any analytics, and you can turn analytics off at any time (see Your choices). When it is on, the iOS and Android apps send these events to GameAnalytics: sessions (start, end, length); matches started and ended (win, loss, or left) with the mode, map, your role (hider or seeker), and score; shop purchases, with the item, its type, and its US-dollar list price (not your receipt or payment details); diamonds and goo earned and spent; purchase errors; crash reports; and device details (model, operating system version, app version). Events are linked to a random identifier that GameAnalytics creates for your installation, together with your device’s vendor identifier (IDFV) on iOS or app set ID on Android. We do not read your advertising identifier on iOS or Android. GameAnalytics receives your IP address with each connection and uses it to estimate your country. We do not request App Tracking Transparency, and we do not send your display name, party codes, installation credential, receipts, paint, or voice to GameAnalytics. We use this information to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs and balance problems. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.
  • This website — GitHub Pages may log standard request metadata (IP, user agent) as part of hosting. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your network request, including your IP address. The site also uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-GZG6MN24PH) to count visits and see which pages people read. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_GZG6MN24PH, which last up to two years by default. Google uses the IP address to estimate a general location, such as country or city, and says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We do not use these reports to identify individual visitors, and we do not run advertising pixels on this site. The invite page (join.html) reports only its address without the party code or invite token.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your data to train unrelated advertising profiles.
  • We do not require a Mecca Gecko account to play the core hide-and-seek loop.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Mecca Gecko is a general-audience party game, not a “Made for Kids” product, and it is not directed primarily to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has sent us personal information or used online features, email us and we will delete what we hold.

4. Who else sees data

  • Apple — App Store, TestFlight, In-App Purchases, Game Center, and crash analytics you choose to share from your device.
  • GameAnalytics — GameAnalytics ApS (Copenhagen, Denmark) and GameAnalytics Limited (London, UK) process the product analytics described above on our behalf, as our service provider. Their data is hosted with Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud. See the GameAnalytics privacy notice.
  • Google Play — distribution, Play Billing, Play services, license checks, and Play’s own install and crash diagnostics for the Android app. Google processes purchases and may process device identifiers required to deliver the store and restore owned items.
  • Other players in your match — your display name, avatar, paint, movement, and voice if you opted in. Players may keep local gameplay replays and share exported videos. Choose a display name and paint you are comfortable sharing.
  • Google Cloud — hosts the multiplayer relay, lobby registry, safety reports, and operational logs.
  • GitHub — hosts this website (GitHub Pages). Google Fonts supplies the website fonts. Google Analytics processes website visit measurements under Google’s privacy policy.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect people from harm.

5. Retention

The relay forwards match traffic without a permanent match or voice recording. Lobby listings become stale after approximately 70 seconds without a host heartbeat; database cleanup runs separately. Safety reports are scheduled to expire after 90 days. Automatic database cleanup is enabled; deletion is asynchronous and may occur after expiration. Enforcement records may remain while a restriction applies. Infrastructure logs and support correspondence may remain after a match ends; standard Google Cloud operational logs are configured for 30 days, while required cloud audit logs have a 400-day retention period. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. Purchase entitlements follow Apple’s and Google Play’s records.

GameAnalytics keeps raw analytics events, including device identifiers, IP addresses, and crash reports, for up to 12 months; we keep aggregated statistics that do not identify you. Google Analytics keeps event-level website data for up to 14 months.

Local replay cleanup keeps up to three recent unpinned replays, subject to a 256 MB storage budget. Replays marked Keep and exported videos remain until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Deleting a replay does not delete an exported video, and deleting an in-app export does not remove a copy saved to Photos, Files, or shared with someone else. Other local saves remain until app data is removed.

6. Your choices

  • Decline microphone access in iOS Settings or in the Android permission prompt and system app settings. Use Settings → General in the game to mute, report, or block a player. The game still plays if you refuse the microphone.
  • Use Settings → Game → Replay recording → Record future matches to enable recording for future matches. Turning it off stops and discards the current recording. A red REC · REPLAY indicator appears while recording. Clip Studio lets you delete saved replays and in-app exported videos. Remove separately saved or shared copies in the destination app or service.
  • Manage Game Center sign-in and privacy in iOS Settings.
  • Restore purchased characters and emotes in Settings → General using the same Apple Account or Google account that bought them. Review purchases in your Apple or Google Play purchase history; diamond packs are consumable and cannot be restored.
  • Email support@valuxgames.com to access, correct, or delete information we hold, or to object / request portability where those rights apply (including EEA/UK residents, and players in Brazil and Mexico: see sections 9 and 10).
  • Turn product analytics off in the one-time notice, or at any time in Settings → Permissions → Gameplay analytics. Turning it off stops new analytics from this device. To have the analytics already linked to your installation deleted, email us. Uninstalling the app also stops all analytics.
  • Block or delete cookies, use a tracker blocker, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. The website works the same with analytics blocked.

Para pais e responsáveis: crianças e adolescentes

O Mecca Gecko tem classificação 9+ na App Store (10 anos no Brasil, autoclassificação) e pode ser usado por crianças e adolescentes. Tratamos os dados deles no seu melhor interesse e não pedimos mais do que o necessário para jogar (art. 14 da LGPD): não há cadastro, e-mail nem nome real.

  • Chat de voz: vem desligado. Para usar, é preciso ligá-lo em Settings → Permissions e permitir o microfone no aparelho; mesmo assim, o microfone começa mudo. Os responsáveis podem impedir o acesso ao microfone pelos controles parentais do aparelho (Tempo de Uso no iPhone e iPad; Family Link no Android).
  • Contato com outros jogadores: outros jogadores veem o nome de exibição, o personagem e a pintura. Não há chat de texto. Qualquer jogador pode silenciar, denunciar ou bloquear outro em Settings → General.
  • Compras: não há caixas de recompensa (loot boxes) nem itens aleatórios pagos; os diamantes compram itens com conteúdo e preço mostrados antes. Os responsáveis podem exigir aprovação de compras com o recurso Pedir para Comprar (Apple) ou com o Family Link (Google).
  • Análise de jogo e replays: a análise pode ser desligada em Settings → Permissions; os replays vêm desligados e ficam no aparelho.
  • Denúncias: para denunciar um jogador ou uma violação aos direitos de crianças e adolescentes, use Report no jogo ou escreva para support@valuxgames.com. Quando uma denúncia indicar crime contra crianças ou adolescentes, nós a comunicamos às autoridades competentes, como a Polícia Federal.
  • Exclusão: os responsáveis podem pedir a exclusão dos dados da criança pelo mesmo e-mail.

Prazos de guarda: seção 5. Os dados trafegam criptografados (HTTPS/TLS).
