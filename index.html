How a round works

Two to four minutes. No radar. Eyes only.

1. Paint

Clone the wallpaper, the sofa, the fruit bowl. Auto Pattern Paint helps you match busy surfaces without being a digital artist.

2. Hide

Hiders shrink to a quarter the seeker’s size. Pose on shelves, in laundry piles, on the rug. Stillness is a skill.

3. Seek

Seekers hunt with toy paint blasters — never realistic guns. Spot the fake among the furniture and splat them.
