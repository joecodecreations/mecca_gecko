Small hider. Big ideas.

Your best disguise is you.

Go solo with bots or bring your friends online. Pick a hiding style, find your spot, and try to keep a straight face.

01 / HIDE AND PAINT

Become the background.

Sample colors and patterns, paint your character, then crouch, curl up, lie flat, or cling to a wall. That painting could use one more gecko.

02 / PROP HUNT

Trust no fruit bowl.

Copy an object in the map and tuck yourself among the real props. Seekers have to spot what doesn’t belong.

03 / RED VS BLUE

Settle it next round.

Play a team series and swap hiding and seeking roles between rounds. Choose Hide and Paint or Prop Hunt for your showdown.
