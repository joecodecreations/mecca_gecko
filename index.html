Small hider. Big ideas.

Four ways to play.

Go solo with bots or bring your friends online. Pick a hiding style, pick a game type, and try to keep a straight face.

HIDE STYLE · HIDE AND PAINT

Become the background.

Sample colors and patterns, paint your character, then crouch, curl up, lie flat or cling to a wall. That painting could use one more gecko.

HIDE STYLE · PROP HUNT

Trust no fruit bowl.

Copy an object in the map and tuck yourself among the real props. Seekers have to spot what doesn’t belong.

GAME TYPE · RED VS BLUE

Settle it next round.

A team series that swaps hiding and seeking between rounds. Play it with Hide and Paint or Prop Hunt.

GAME TYPE · INFECTION NEW

Get tagged, join the hunt.

Every hider who gets hit becomes a seeker. Survive to the buzzer, or infect everyone.

What’s new

Built for game night.

Everything you need to get the group together, keep it friendly and save the best moments.

🔗

Invite links

Tap COPY INVITE LINK in your lobby and send it anywhere. Friends tap it to open the game straight into your party.

🔒

Locked rooms

Add a four-digit PIN so only people you invite can join by code. You can also fill the room with humans only.

🎬

Game Clips & Clip Studio

Turn on replay recording, then trim a match, move the camera and export a 9:16, square or 16:9 video to share. Recording starts off.

🏆

Game Center

Chase achievements and climb the Best Score, Weekly, Hiders Found and Clean Escapes leaderboards. Sign-in is optional.

🎙️

Optional voice

Talk to your party with proximity voice. The mic starts muted, and mute, report and block are one tap away in Settings.

🤖

Bots fill the room

Two friends or just you? Bots take the empty spots so there’s always a full match, online or offline.

Your party. Your choices.

Play comfortably.

Voice is optional and the mic starts muted. Mute, report or block players in Settings during a party, and lock your room with a PIN. You don’t need a separate Mecca Gecko account.

Online players can see each other’s names and paint and hear live voice. Parents may want to look at these features before their family plays.
