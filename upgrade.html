Shop and purchases

Mecca Gecko is free to download and play. All launch maps are included. Optional purchases are billed by Apple at the localized price shown in the app.

Characters and emotes

Individual character skins and emotes are permanent non-consumable purchases. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account after reinstalling or changing devices.

Diamonds

Diamond packs are consumable purchases. Diamonds can be spent in the in-game shop on emotes and single-use powerups. Powerups can change a round's abilities; rounds using them are excluded from public Game Center scoring.

Diamond balances and items acquired with diamonds are stored on the device. Consumable packs cannot be restored from Apple after reinstalling or moving to another device. Review the in-app item description before buying.

No map upgrade or subscription

The former map Upgrade is not offered by the current game. You do not need to buy a map pass or subscription to play the launch maps.

Restore

New phone? Same Apple ID? Use Settings → General → Restore Purchases for eligible Apple-purchased characters and emotes. Diamond packs and items bought with diamonds are not restored by Apple. Still missing? Support.

Refunds: Apple, via reportaproblem.apple.com.
